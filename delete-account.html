Delete Your Vudu Notify Account

This page explains how users of Vudu Notify can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to Vudu Notify support using the button below. Use the email address associated with your Vudu Notify account.

Request account deletion

Please include the following information:

  • Your full name
  • The email address or username associated with your account
  • Your organization name, if applicable
  • A clear request to delete your Vudu Notify account

Data that will be deleted

After the request has been verified and approved, the account and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized, including:

  • Account profile information
  • Authentication information
  • Device and notification identifiers
  • Other personal data associated with the account

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, contractual, security, auditing, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes. Information may also be retained in backups for a limited period before being securely removed.

Account deletion is permanent. After deletion, access to Vudu Notify and associated information may not be recoverable.

Processing time

We will review the request and may contact the user to verify their identity before deleting the account.
