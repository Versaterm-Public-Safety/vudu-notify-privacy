Last updated: July 24, 2026

Depending on the features used, Vudu Notify may process account information, notification information, messages, device identifiers, technical information, camera or microphone content, and location information.

Information is used to authenticate users, deliver notifications, provide messaging features, operate the application, improve performance, maintain security, and provide technical support.

Information may be processed by authorized service providers that support the operation, hosting, security, and notification features of the application. Information is not shared except as required to provide the service, comply with the law, or protect the security of users and the application.

4. Data security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application, meet contractual requirements, comply with legal obligations, and maintain security.

Users may contact the organization to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.
